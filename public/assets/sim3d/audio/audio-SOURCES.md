## Provenance and checks

Retrieved on 2026-08-30. Wikimedia's individual file pages identify both the
British accent and CC BY 3.0 US license. Direct Wikimedia downloads timed out
on the development host, so the files were downloaded from Yojik's preserved
copy of the original Shtooka collection, not from an unrelated pronunciation
service.

The preserved original
[Shtooka README](https://fsi-languages.yojik.eu/audiocollections/detailled/eng-balm-judith/readme.txt)
also names Judith Franck, credits Association Shtooka, and links to the same
CC BY 3.0 US license. The collection's
[word-to-file index](https://fsi-languages.yojik.eu/audiocollections/detailled/eng-balm-judith/ogg/index.tags.txt)
maps `milk` and `bread` to the filenames listed above.

Local checks verified the Ogg/Vorbis header, sample rate, channel count,
complete Ogg page boundaries, final sample counts, and embedded `SWAC_TEXT`,
`SWAC_SPEAK_NAME`, and license/copyright fields. Browser playback is tested
separately in the application.

Only the individual words are supplied. These files do not contain the phrases
“a carton of milk”, “a loaf of bread”, or “Card, please”. Do not present their
playback as recordings of those full phrases.

Suggested compact credit for an app credits screen:

> “milk” and “bread” — Judith Franck / Association Shtooka, © 2009,
> CC BY 3.0 US; original recordings unchanged. Sources: Wikimedia Commons
> and the Shtooka collection preserved by Yojik.

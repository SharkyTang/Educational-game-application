# Customer corpus: local female and male speech

The 70 WAV files under `customers/female/` and `customers/male/` are **synthetic
speech**, generated locally on 2026-08-31 from the game's 35 original everyday
shop phrases. They are not human recordings of those lines, not macOS system
voices, and not output from an online or paid speech service. The female and
male sets use distinct pretrained neural speaker identities, not pitch-shifted
or speed-adjusted copies of one voice.

## Reviewed voice sources

Both models come directly from [Bryce Beattie's official Piper voice page](https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/),
checked on 2026-08-31. The author labels **Cori medium** and **ManyVoice** as
**public domain**, describes LibriVox training sources, and permits legal and
ethical use without a research-only or noncommercial restriction.

- `female`: **Cori medium**, the author's single-speaker UK-English female model.
  [Weights](https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/cori-med.onnx) ·
  [configuration](https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/cori-med.onnx.json).
- `male`: **ManyVoice**, using its independent `Martin_Clifton` voice at
  **speaker ID 15**. The author describes this model as containing US and UK
  voices; its [official configuration](https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/mv2.onnx.json)
  identifies this speaker. [Weights](https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/mv2.onnx).
  This British-English speaker was selected for the male customers; it is not
  the Cori voice with a changed playback rate. The underlying LibriVox reader's
  [catalogue](https://librivox.org/reader/31) is provided for provenance, not as
  an endorsement or a claim that the reader performed the game's dialogue.

The separate model permission is the basis for using these synthesized assets.
No model author or source speaker endorses this fictional shop. This source
record does not waive anyone's personality or moral rights. The existing human
`milk.ogg` and `bread.ogg` retain their separate credits in `audio-SOURCES.md`;
they are not generated by, or attributed to, either customer voice. The eight
legacy WAV files and `phrase-manifest.json` are unchanged.

## Shipped files and authoring dependencies

`src/sim3d/customerCorpus.json` is the single editable text source. It contains
8 greetings, 12 card-payment lines, 8 post-payment thanks, 4 cash-payment
requests and 3 cash-tender lines, each with an English caption, Chinese
translation and stable kebab-case ID. The cash request pool is selected only
for visitors with a saved cash payment. Tender lines describe the saved £5 or
£10 amount; other exact tenders use "I have the exact amount." instead of
constructing unsupported spoken numbers. Each phrase has
both `/assets/sim3d/audio/customers/female/{id}.wav` and
`/assets/sim3d/audio/customers/male/{id}.wav`. The separate
`customer-phrase-manifest.json` records the source JSON hash and all clip text,
paths, sizes, durations, hashes and levels; it is generated, not hand-edited.

Generation uses [Piper 1.7.0](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl/tree/v1.7.0)
and its [Python API](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl/blob/v1.7.0/docs/API_PYTHON.md),
with `onnxruntime==1.19.2` and `numpy==2.0.2`. Piper's code is GPL-3.0-or-later;
the models have the separate source permissions above. The browser game ships
only newly synthesized audio, not Piper, eSpeak, ONNX Runtime, model weights or
training recordings. Authoring dependencies stay in ignored `tmp/` directories.

## Reproduce or extend the corpus

Reuse the isolated `tmp/piper-env` described in `phrase-SOURCES.md`. Download the
models directly from the author's site if they are not already present:

```sh
mkdir -p tmp/piper-models
curl -fL --retry 2 -o tmp/piper-models/cori-med.onnx https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/cori-med.onnx
curl -fL --retry 2 -o tmp/piper-models/cori-med.onnx.json https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/cori-med.onnx.json
curl -fL --retry 2 -o tmp/piper-models/mv2.onnx https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/mv2.onnx
curl -fL --retry 2 -o tmp/piper-models/mv2.onnx.json https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/mv2.onnx.json
tmp/piper-env/bin/python scripts/generate-customer-speech.py --reuse-existing
npm test -- tests/unit/sim3d-customer-speech-assets.test.ts
```

The script checks these exact, reviewed input hashes before loading a model:

| --- | ---: | --- |

| `cori-med.onnx.json` | 7,087 | `4e4e862c06be95e268cb2edeb18eed39b4fc51c515d808ed3c51404f20e96fea` |
| `mv2.onnx` | 77,100,103 | `3cc7b8f55b82146c09265e909beaa539254a882b9a6ac915792cbfc7d41ba235` |
| `mv2.onnx.json` | 7,528 | `1dae6569843412ace557acdc2f2136405219ab75219efb3ddf882af2055a4c1c` |

To add phrases, edit the appropriate scene in `customerCorpus.json` and rerun
the generator for **both** voices. `--reuse-existing` checks existing clip text,
voice settings, size and hash against the previous manifest, then generates
only new clips. Translation-only changes refresh the Chinese manifest caption
without changing the English recording. The cash extension added 14 clips
while leaving all 56 existing customer WAV files byte-for-byte unchanged.
The eight legacy phrase WAV files
and their separate `phrase-manifest.json` also remain untouched.

Omit `--reuse-existing` for an explicitly reviewed full regeneration or an
empty output directory. Do not change an existing ID to mean a different
phrase without regenerating its audio. Keep card/cash requests compatible with
the saved payment choice; bags, receipts, refunds and discounts are not
implemented. Test and review the resulting manifest and WAV files together.
The script does not delete retired files automatically; incremental generation
rejects retired or altered existing spoken-text records so removal needs an
explicit, reviewed change and orphaned assets are not silently lost.

## Audio preparation and verification limits

Output is RIFF/WAVE, mono 22,050 Hz signed 16-bit PCM. Synthesis uses seed 31,
length scale 1.18 for Cori and 1.12 for ManyVoice, noise scale 0.667 and noise
width 0.8. A 0.18-second gap separates sentences. Conservative edge-silence
trimming, 4 ms fades, at most 2.5x gain and a -3 dBFS peak ceiling follow the
legacy authoring tool's method. Spoken-word timing is not edited afterward.

The generator validates the complete batch before writing published files. It
rejects silence, invalid formats, clips outside 0.3-6 seconds and a combined
download of 8,000,000 bytes or more. Unit tests independently verify the corpus,
both complete path mappings, RIFF chunk sizes, PCM headers, hashes, levels,
budget and unchanged legacy assets. Native-library/platform differences can
change regenerated samples; hashes identify the shipped assets, not a promise
of bit-identical synthesis on every platform. Automated integrity checks do not
replace human feedback on pronunciation, accent and voice naturalness.
